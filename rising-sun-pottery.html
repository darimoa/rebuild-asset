01 The studio

Half a century at the potter's wheel.

Rising Sun Pottery has been a fixture of downtown Lincolnton for roughly fifty years — a working studio where the smell of wet clay and the hum of the wheel have never really stopped since about 1973.

We are a full-service studio in three parts: a teaching studio where new potters learn the craft, a retail gallery of finished work, and a supply and equipment dealer for potters across the region. The work that leaves here is utilitarian and folk-inspired — wheel-thrown pots and hand-built pieces alike.

Steeped in 200 years of North Carolina's Catawba Valley pottery tradition — from utilitarian ware to roosters and face jugs. Rising Sun Pottery · Lincolnton, NC

That heritage shapes how we teach. Classes stay small — never more than five students — so every pair of hands gets real attention at the wheel. It is the same way the craft has always been passed on: patiently, one pot at a time.

04 Gallery & supply

Folk-art pottery & a real supply house.

The gallery is stocked with wheel-thrown and hand-built work in the Catawba Valley spirit — utilitarian jugs and bowls alongside folk-art pieces like roosters and face jugs, each one made in-studio.

We are also a working supply house: clay, dry materials, tools, glazes, kilns and equipment at retail and wholesale, with kiln sales and equipment repair on hand.
