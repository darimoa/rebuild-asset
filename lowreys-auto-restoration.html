Three Generations in Tilton

A Shop Built On Steel & Family

It started in 1960, when Jim Lowrey Sr. began working from home before opening a collision shop in Chelmsford, Massachusetts. His son, Jim Lowrey Jr., picked up a wrench at fourteen and took over in 1987 — refocusing the shop entirely on antique restoration and award-winning hot rod construction.

Today the shop sits on a former dairy farm in Tilton, New Hampshire: a three-level barn for storage alongside dedicated buildings for fabrication, paint and final assembly. Jim Sr. still rebuilds flathead Ford motors. It stays a small, tight crew — Jim, Dave, Joe, Joe N., and Debbie running the office.
