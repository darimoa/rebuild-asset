The Craft

From sketch to gold leaf

A hand-carved sign is a small commission of its own. Here is how each one comes together in the Adel Signs shop.

1

Design

We draw your sign by hand — lettering, layout, and shape drafted to fit your business and your building.

2

Hand-carve

The design is carved into solid wood with hand tools, giving each letter real depth and shadow.

3

Gold leaf

Lettering is painted and, where you'd like it, finished in genuine gold leaf that catches the light for years.

4

Install

Your finished sign is sealed for the New England weather and ready to hang where the whole town can see it.

Our Story

A Lakes Region sign shop since 1978

Adel Signs has been carving and painting signs in Laconia, New Hampshire for more than thirty-five years. Over that time we've lettered storefronts, carved tavern signs, and gold-leafed nameplates for neighbors across Laconia, Belmont, Concord, and Gilford.

It's a craft that hasn't changed much — and that's the point. When a sign is drawn, carved, and finished by hand, it carries a warmth that machine-cut work never will. That's what we've built our name on, one sign at a time.
