Our story

A family trade, passed down and perfected.

"Established in 1982 with 16 years of upholstery already behind us — today that's more than 50 years in the craft."

Founder Sam started the shop with his wife Alice on the books and his brother Issa cutting and sewing. Cousin Ayman and Mohammed joined as the shop grew. Four decades later, Moorestown Auto & Boat Upholstery is still a family business — and still doing every job by hand.
