04 — The shop

CNC precision, backed by manual hands.

05 — About

Built by a benchrest competitor.

Precision Barrel Work was founded in the early 1990s by owner Pete Pieper, who came up through benchrest competition — the discipline where a few thousandths of an inch separate winning from watching.

That background sets the standard for every rifle and every barrel that leaves the shop. Nothing goes out that Pete wouldn't shoot in a match himself.

"Success in Benchrest Competition necessitates strict adherence to minimal tolerance and painstaking devotion to detail."
— Pete Pieper, Owner
