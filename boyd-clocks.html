Repair & restoration for every kind of timepiece

Our story

Eighty years, one address, one craft.

Boyd Clocks has kept time for Tampa Bay since 1944. Through three generations the shop has stayed exactly what it started as — a place where a clock is opened up, understood, and set right by hand. Owner David Boyd still runs the bench at 11620 North Florida Avenue.
