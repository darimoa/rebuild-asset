What Bright's does

Three ways to bring a machine back.

Detailing

Non-destructive cleaning that preserves original finishes — the honest patina collectors prize, brought back without stripping history away.

Repair

Mechanism work from a minor tune-up to a complete teardown — coin heads, reels, timing and payout brought back to reliable working order.

Full Restoration

Proper refinishing of every metal and wood surface, mechanical rebuild, and the correct castings — a machine returned to showroom condition.

Signature · The restoration journey

From tired relic to ringing again.

Every machine that comes through the shop follows the same honest path — and leaves with a guarantee behind it.

I

Detail

Careful, non-destructive cleaning to see exactly what the machine has, and to preserve every original finish worth keeping.

II

Repair

The mechanism is diagnosed and rebuilt — from a light tune-up to a full teardown — until it runs the way it did on day one.

III

Restore

Metal and woodwork are refinished, correct parts fitted, and the machine reassembled to display-and-play condition.

Backed for life

Every machine Bright's restores carries a lifetime repair guarantee. When one craftsman has done it for thirty-five years, he stands behind the work.

The craftsman
"I've been restoring antique slot machines for over thirty-five years — at least five hundred in my lifetime."
— Richard Bright, Bright's Antique Slot Machine Restorations

Between twenty-four-hour shifts as a firefighter, Richard rebuilt his first machine — and never stopped. What began in a home workshop became a reputation among collectors up and down the East Coast for machines that don't just look right, but ring, spin and pay out exactly as they did a century ago.

Mills. Jennings. Watling. Pace. Caille. If it's an antique one-armed bandit, it belongs on his bench.
