Trophy care

The mount starts in the field.

A great mount begins with how you handle your trophy before it reaches the studio. A few simple steps make all the difference.

01 / FIELD

Keep it cool & clean

Get fish and game cool quickly, keep the cape and fins clean, and avoid dragging or cuts through the shoulder area.

02 / FREEZE

Wrap & freeze

Wrap fish in a wet towel and bag them; freeze birds and capes to lock in detail until you can bring them in.

03 / BRING IT IN

Talk it through

Bring your trophy by the studio and we'll plan the pose, habitat and finish that fits the memory.
