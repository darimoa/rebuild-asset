The signature · Lead‑free

Structural Stained Glass©

Traditional leaded windows are beautiful — but the lead came softens and bows over generations. Our Structural Stained Glass© system holds the same artistry in a lead‑free structure built to stand up to weather and time.

  • Lead‑free — engineered for long‑term durability
  • The look of traditional glass, without the sag
  • Custom designed for each church and opening
  • Backed by a studio that has done this since 1927
Restoration & relocation

Glass worth saving, saved.

When a church is renovated, sold, or simply showing its age, the windows don't have to be lost. We remove, restore and relocate historic stained glass — re‑leading, repairing and re‑hanging it so it keeps telling its story in a new generation's sanctuary.
