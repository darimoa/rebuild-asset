The workroom

Started with a needle at twelve. Built into a Chicago institution.

The shop grew out of a hands-on love of the craft — an engineer's precision brought to the patience of upholstery. Today the workroom takes on everything from a single dining chair to a full room of custom drapery, all finished in-house by people who do this every day.

"Their craftsmanship and attention to detail brought new life to furniture our parents loved."

— Richard Clegg, customer review
