Our Story

Family-owned formalwear for over 25 years

Elegant Touch Bridal and Tuxedo is a family-owned, full-service bridal and tuxedo shop — a pioneer in the formal wear industry for over 25 years, and the Baltimore Metro Area’s premier tuxedo shop. From the first gown fitting to the groom’s final tux, we are a one-stop destination for the entire wedding party.
