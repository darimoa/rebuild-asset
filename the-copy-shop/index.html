Since 1982

The Rickard family
Est.1982Traverse City

Three copiers, one family, and a whole lot of ink. Forty-plus years of getting it right.

The Copy Shop opened in June 1982 — Jim Rickard brought the Xerox know-how, Kay Rickard brought the care for every customer who walked in. Together they were first in Traverse City to offer high-speed black & white and the area's first color laser copier.

In 1999 their son Dan took the reins, and the shop has kept the same promise ever since: more than copies — real solutions, printed to look exactly how you imagined them.
