Signature · Neon Rescue

Is your sign trying to tell you something?

Old neon rarely dies all at once — it warns you first. Here's what the symptoms usually mean, before it goes dark for good.

Flickering
Likely

An aging transformer or a loose electrode connection — usually a straightforward fix if caught early.

Buzzing
Likely

The transformer is failing. Swapping it out protects the tubes and quiets the sign back down.

Dim / pink
Likely

Gas or vacuum loss in the tube. Often re-gassed or re-pumped rather than replaced outright.

Dead section
Likely

A cracked tube or bad electrode. We re-bend and re-fit that segment to match the original glow.
