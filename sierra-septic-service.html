How a service call works

Simple, honest, and no surprises

From your first call to a clean bill of health, here's exactly what to expect when you work with us.

1

Call us

Tell us what's happening. We ask the right questions, give straight answers, and schedule a visit that works for you.

2

We inspect — camera & video

We locate the tank and run a camera through the lines so we diagnose the real problem before anyone starts digging.

3

Pump & clean

We pump the tank and clear the lines with auger or hydro-flush — leaving the system flowing freely and your yard clean.

4

Report & repair

You get a clear report on your system's condition and honest recommendations — and if a repair is needed, we handle it.

About us

Three generations of the Patton family

Sierra Septic Service was founded in 1981 by Wayne Patton along with his two sons, Scott and Robert. What began as a family business has stayed one ever since.

Today Scott Patton owns and operates the company, carrying it forward with the same values it was built on — treating every customer's home and business the way we'd want our own treated.
