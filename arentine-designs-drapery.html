A workroom, not a showroom

Marie has been sewing soft treatments here since 1989.

Arentine Designs is independently owned and operated by Marie, who works on-site every day. This is a genuine custom soft-goods workroom — drapery, shades, bedding and upholstery are cut, sewn and finished right here using first-quality linings and interlinings.

“We’re not a franchise or just selling other workroom’s work.”

For more than 35 years she has partnered with Dallas–Fort Worth homeowners and interior designers to fabricate window treatments made to hang beautifully and last. Because everything is made to order, the workroom is by appointment only — email or call to schedule.

What we fabricate

A full-service soft-treatment workroom

From first-quality drapery to upholstery, bedding and motorization — all crafted and installed with a tailor’s eye.

Custom Drapery & Curtains

In-house fabricated panels, pinch-pleat drapery, swags & cascades, top treatments, cornices and valances using first-quality linings and interlinings — pattern-placement and design guidance included.

Roman & Soft Shades

Custom fabric roman shades and relaxed / soft shades made to order for a clean, tailored window.

Blinds, Shutters & Hard Treatments

Plantation shutters, blinds, roller shades and woven-wood shades to complement your soft treatments.

Custom Bedding & Pillows

Coverlets, duvets, shams and decorative pillows coordinated to the room for a finished, layered look.

Upholstery & Reupholstery

Furniture, cushions and headboards recovered and rebuilt in the workroom, right down to the trim.

Outdoor / Patio Treatments

Patio screens and durable outdoor-fabric treatments that carry your style beyond the walls.

Decorative Hardware & Trimmings

Drapery rods, finials, tapes and trims selected to finish and elevate every treatment.

Motorization

Motorized and automated window-treatment options for effortless, modern control.

Full-Service Workroom & Installation

In-home measuring, quoting, fabric consultation, professional installation, and removal of old treatments.

By appointment — how we work

A workroom, not a store

Arentine Designs is by appointment only — not a retail showroom and not open to the public. Here is how a project comes together.

1

Consult

Email or call to schedule. We discuss your rooms, fabrics, patterns, linings and the look you want.

2

Measure

In-home measuring and a detailed quote, with design and pattern-placement guidance from Marie.

3

Fabricate in-house

Your treatments are cut, sewn and finished here in the workroom using first-quality linings and interlinings.

4

Install

Professional installation — Marie is present at the install — plus removal of your old treatments.

Please note: we are open by appointment only. Email or call to schedule a consultation — we look forward to meeting you.
