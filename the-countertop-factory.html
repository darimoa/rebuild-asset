The Countertop Factory Advantage

Fabricated on-site, right here in Raleigh.

A one-stop shop for marble & granite countertops, built on over 20 years of experience managing countertop businesses.

We custom-manufacture countertops on-site for residential and commercial jobs of any size across central North Carolina.
