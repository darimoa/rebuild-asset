The living art

Patience, shaped into something you can hold.

The art of bonsai as we know it traces back nearly two thousand years — carried from China through Korea and Japan, shaped by Buddhist thought into a quiet practice of tending, trimming and waiting. A bonsai isn't a product that's finished; it's a relationship that grows.

"We help you begin — and then the tree does the rest."

Care by season

A tree keeps time with the year.

春 Spring
Awaken

Repot before buds break, begin feeding, and prune new growth to set the season's shape.

夏 Summer
Tend

Water daily in heat, pinch back vigor, and shade tender species from the harshest sun.

秋 Autumn
Slow

Ease off feeding, let deciduous trees color, and wire branches while they're supple.

冬 Winter
Rest

Protect roots from freeze, water sparingly, and let the tree take the quiet it needs.
