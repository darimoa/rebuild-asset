Signature · Mail-in restoration

Send us your music box.

Near or far, an heirloom can come back to song. Here's how a mail-in restoration works — and exactly what we bring back to life.

1 · Describe

Email us what you have — maker, size, what's stopped working — with a photo or two. We'll reply with an approach and estimate.

2 · Ship

Pack it snug and send it to Dade City. We handle boxes from around the world every year — the trip is routine.

3 · Restore

On our own bench, by hand — then back to you playing the way it did the day it was made.

Our story
"Formerly the Port Washington Music House — the same hands, the same care, six decades on."

The Music House began in 1965 in Port Washington, New York, and has spent every year since among cylinders, combs and cases. Today, from Dade City, Florida, we ship music boxes and musical gifts across the United States, Canada and the world — and we still fix them ourselves, one at a time.
