What we build & repair

Services from the clinic

Custom Fan Shrouds

Fabricated aluminum shrouds matched to your radiator and fan for maximum airflow and a clean engine-bay look.

04

Engine, Radiator & A/C Fans

Specialty electric and mechanical fans specified and installed to keep temperatures in check on any build.

Heater Core Recoring

Walnut, glass bead, aluminum oxide and plastic media blasting to strip and prep parts the right way.

Our story

Built by Ray & Sylvia since 1982

Ray Ruminski apprenticed in the radiator trade for roughly ten years before he and his wife Sylvia opened Ray's Radiator Clinic in 1982. Gas-tank relining came in 1988, and in 1999 the shop pivoted to custom aluminum radiators and fuel cells under the "Dreams & Reality" line.

A dedicated building followed in 2001, and in 2016 the clinic relocated to its current Sterling Heights home. More than 40 years on, it's still a family shop that fabricates, welds, powder-coats and blasts everything in-house.
